## More facts... what would you like to have answered?

_WBS INFO · Sep 8, 2009|42_

> CHAPTER 3
>
> The M460LA Input Module (14")
>
> The M460 series of input modules had numerous subscripts running from M460 to M460M. Because of the application of M460 modules to almost 100% custom consoles, if WBS were able to use an exsisting M460 design, they did. If not, they knocked off a new M460 and gave it the next available subscript letter. M460LA is a coincidence. Another was the M460C - used for consoles provided to CBS-TV.
>
> As the company grew...
>
> Ron and Roger eyes were always on every project. Obviously every customer did not require a custom design, particularly as the company grew and "standard" console designs were implemented - M1203, M1204, L2042, L3242, R1000, R1200, R2000, and T1202. WBS had a Systems Engineering Dept., which grew to about 10 people, to implement small to moderate customization using existing modular elements. The company reached 110 people at it's peak, in two buildings, at 841 Progress Ave., Scarborough.

> All M460 series modules are pretty much the same for current draw, and out-put. The only odd duck was the M460M. It was grudgingly designed by Engineering in building 1 and they made a huge mess of it. 
>
> Please go to the manuals section that Tony has assembled for us and make reference to any M460 module there. Either use the direct out or the fader out. You will have TONNES of headroom and gain. Most likely too much, and you should perhaps pad the out-put. Tom successfully sells racked M460's to many, many happy clients world wide, and I use M460's in desk-top towers in my studio almost daily.
>
> The designations after the module number typically referred to a customers specific needs for EQ configuration, assignment, sends, no pre-amplifyer, etc...

> You are just running 8 modules? 1.5 amps - 2 amps should be fine. But more is always better. Be careful as to the transformer more so than the regulator you choose. Are you going to do your own thing or use a JLM kit? Upon Tom's recommendation I retied a JLM kit for a current project of mine.
